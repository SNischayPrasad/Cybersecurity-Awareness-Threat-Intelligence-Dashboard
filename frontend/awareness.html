Incident response awareness

A high-level lifecycle (based on common frameworks such as NIST SP 800-61; other frameworks use different terms, e.g. SANS PICERL):

Preparation→Detection / Identification→ Containment→Eradication→ Recovery→Lessons learned

Everyone's checklist

  • Know how to report: report button, helpdesk number, security email.
  • Report quickly — minutes matter more than certainty.
  • Write down what happened, when, and on which device/account.
  • Disconnect a suspected infected device from the network (unplug / Wi-Fi off). Do not wipe or power it off unless told to.
  • Do not delete suspicious emails or files — they are evidence.
  • Do not contact, "hack back" or investigate the attacker yourself.
  • Follow the response team's instructions and keep details confidential.

Organisation's checklist (educational)

  • Preparation: contacts list, playbooks, tested backups, logging, tabletop exercises.
  • Detection: triage alerts, confirm scope, open an incident ticket, preserve evidence.
  • Containment: isolate affected hosts/accounts with approval; reset exposed credentials.
  • Eradication: remove the root cause (patch, remove persistence) under change control.
  • Recovery: restore from known-good backups, monitor closely for recurrence.
  • Lessons learned: blameless review, update controls, training and detection rules.
