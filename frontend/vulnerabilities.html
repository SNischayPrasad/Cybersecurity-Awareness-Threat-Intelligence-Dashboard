CVE & vulnerability awareness

Contextual vulnerability prioritisation

A vulnerability is a weakness in software. A CVE ID is its public name. CVSS rates its technical severity (0–10) — but CVSS does not know your environment. Priority here combines CVSS + asset criticality + exposure + exploitation evidence + business context.
