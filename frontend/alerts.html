SOC workflow

Alert queue & triage

Alerts are generated from risk/confidence thresholds, repeated observations, correlated clusters and high-priority vulnerabilities — then correlated so duplicates become one alert with an observation count.

Tier 1 SOC investigation workflow

Threat feed→IOC detected→ Validation→Enrichment→ Risk + confidence→Alert→ SOC queue→Analyst triage→ Correlation→Investigation→ Escalate / Monitor / Resolve / False positive→Documentation

Tier 1 analysts work the queue: confirm the alert is valid, enrich it, check related records, decide (escalate to Tier 2, monitor, resolve, or mark false positive) and document the reasoning in analyst notes.
