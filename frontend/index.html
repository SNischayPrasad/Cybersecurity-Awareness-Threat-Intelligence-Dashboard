Evidence ladder — not everything suspicious is an attack

OBSERVATION
—

A raw sighting: something was seen in a log or feed.

INDICATOR
—

A validated artifact (IP, domain, URL, hash) with context.

ALERT
—

A rule fired: this deserves an analyst's attention.

THREAT
—

Triage judged it credible and relevant to us.

INCIDENT
—

Confirmed impact, declared by an analyst. None in demo data.

Intelligence pipeline

Every record flows through the same defensive pipeline:

Synthetic feed→ Collection→ Normalisation→ Validation→ IOC extraction→ Classification→ Risk + confidence→ ATT&CK mapping (if justified)→ Threat DB→ Correlation→ SOC dashboard→ Awareness→ Analyst / User

Risk is not confidence

Risk score
How concerning the indicator could be (severity, recency, frequency, context).
Confidence score
How much we trust the intelligence (source reliability, corroboration, validation).
Risk 90 · Conf 25
Potentially serious, but evidence is weak — validate first.
Risk 70 · Conf 95
High-confidence intelligence with meaningful risk — triage.
